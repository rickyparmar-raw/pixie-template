# How to Start with YSWS (You Ship, We Ship) at Hack Club

## What is YSWS?

**YSWS (You Ship, We Ship)** is Hack Club's flagship program where learning by doing pays off—literally. The philosophy is simple: **"You build a project (you ship), submit it, and you get rewarded with amazing real-world hardware, grants, and cool swag (we ship)."**

YSWS is funded by charitable donations to The Hack Foundation (501c3 nonprofit, EIN: 81-2908499) and rewards teenagers for building real-world projects by shipping them high-quality gear, hardware, or financial grants in return.

## Who Can Participate?

- **Age requirement:** You must be **13-18 years old** (high school or younger)
- **No gap year required:** Any teenager can participate in active YSWS programs at any time—completely free of charge
- **Global:** Open to participants worldwide (international shipping available, though customs fees may apply)
- **Homeschool students welcome:** Homeschooled students and early high school leavers are fully eligible

## How YSWS Works

The process follows a clean four-step loop:

1. **Choose a Program:** Browse active YSWS programs on the [YSWS Catalog](https://ysws.hackclub.com)
2. **Build and Track Time:** Write code, design a circuit board, or create your project while tracking your hours using verification tools
3. **Submit Your Ship:** Provide a link to your public GitHub repository, a live demo, and proof of your tracked hours
4. **Get Your Reward:** Once reviewed and approved, Hack Club ships your hardware or issues a grant via an HCB virtual credit card

## Getting Started - Required Setup

Before you start building, you need to set up your verification pipeline:

### 1. Join Hack Club Slack

- Sign up at https://hackclub.com/slack
- Join the global community of 27,000+ teen makers
- Access dedicated channels for each YSWS program (#gemini, #axiom, #enclosure, #electronics, etc.)

### 2. Verify Your Student Status

- Go to `identity.hackclub.com` and verify yourself
- You'll need proof of enrollment: school ID card or official enrollment documentation
- This verification is required before you can submit projects

### 3. Set Up Time Tracking (CRUCIAL)

You **must** log your progress to verify the effort you put into your project:

- **For Software Projects:** Use **Hackatime** (Hack Club's custom WakaTime integration)
  - Set up Hackatime and connect it to your code editor
  - It automatically tracks your coding time
- Different programs have different minimum hours (e.g., Axiom: 8 hours, Gemini: 30 hours, Penumbra: 4 hours)

- **For Hardware/CAD Projects:** Use **Lapse and Lookout** to record timelapses of your design and assembly process

### 4. Make Your Project Open Source

- All projects must be open-source and hosted on GitHub
- Maintain clean, well-documented repositories with thorough README files
- Make frequent, descriptive commits (every 1 hour or so) to show visible progress

## Submission Requirements

When you're ready to ship:

### What You Need:

1. **GitHub Repository:** Open-source code with frequent, descriptive commits
2. **Live Demo:** Deployed website, published app, or documented hardware project
3. **Time Tracking:** Verified hours logged via Hackatime (software) or Lapse/Lookout (hardware)
4. **Project Documentation:** Thorough README explaining what you built and how it works
5. **Original Work:** Your design must be unique, functional, and built by you

### Submission Process:

1. **Submit via Form or PR:**
   - Some programs use submission forms (include GitHub link, live demo, Hackatime proof)
   - Hardware programs like OnBoard use Pull Requests to the main repository

2. **Dual Review:**
   - **Engineering Review:** Verifies your design is functional, unique, and follows program guidelines
   - **Enrollment Review:** Confirms your student verification documents
   - Reviews typically take 24-48 business hours

3. **Approval & Fulfillment:**
   - Once approved, your PR is merged or grant is authorized
   - You'll receive an email with an HCB virtual credit card or shipping notification

## Tips for Getting Approved

### ✅ DO:

- **Commit frequently:** Make descriptive commits every 1 hour or so to show visible progress
- **Provide a working live demo:** Reviewers must be able to interact with your project
- **Be specific about what you want:** Connect your reward request to what you'll build next
- **Ship consistently:** The more you build and share, the more you get
- **Ask questions:** Use Slack channels for help—mentors are there to support you

### ❌ DON'T:

- **Use AI-generated code:** Some programs explicitly ban AI tools; reviewers look for genuine, human-engineered work
- **Submit school assignments:** Projects you had to do anyway aren't eligible
- **Fake your hours:** Time drastically disproportionate to what the project should take will be reduced during audit
- **Copy others' work:** Must be your original creation
- **Attempt to defraud:** Fraud takes money from other teenagers; you'll be removed from the community

## Common Questions

**Q: How long does approval take?**
A: Usually 24-48 business hours after submission

**Q: Can I submit multiple projects?**
A: Yes! Ship often, get supplies often. It's a cycle.

**Q: What if my project is small/simple?**
A: Ship it anyway! Everyone starts somewhere, and shipping anything > shipping nothing

**Q: Do I need to be in the US?**
A: Hack Club ships internationally, but you may be responsible for customs fees (check beforehand)

**Q: Can I participate in multiple YSWS programs at once?**
A: Yes! Each program is independent—just submit a project for each one

**Q: What if I can't double-dip with another program?**
A: Ask the respective program hosts—some allow it, some don't

**Q: What if my project gets rejected?**
A: You'll receive feedback on why. You may be able to make adjustments and resubmit

## The YSWS Mindset

This isn't about getting free stuff—it's about:

- **Building a habit of shipping**
- **Learning by doing**
- **Sharing what you make**
- **Supporting your next project**
- **Being part of a maker community**

The goal is to get you into a cycle: **build → ship → get supplies → build again.**
